What we pretend to cover

These are plain-language descriptions of common commercial insurance types. They are examples for the demo, not products you can buy.

General liability
Pays when a customer or visitor says your business caused injury or damage to them.
Property
Covers buildings, equipment, and stock after fire, storms, theft, and similar events.
Cyber
Helps with the costs of a data breach, ransomware, or a long outage of your systems.
Workers' compensation
Covers medical bills and lost wages when an employee is hurt on the job.
Commercial auto
Covers vehicles used for business, including the people driving them.
Management liability
Protects leaders from claims about how they run the organization.

How the blueprint works

The agent runs the whole process in a chat. It uses a short knowledge file and three skills: scoring, improvement plan, and renewal timeline.

  1. Answer ten questions

    Industry, size, claims, safety, cyber, vendors, renewal date, and your main goal. One question at a time.

  2. Get a score from 0 to 100

    Five categories are scored from 1 to 5 and weighted. Higher means stronger.

  3. Read your plan

    Two or three strengths, three to five improvements ranked by priority, and how they fit your goal.

  4. Work back from renewal day

    Dated milestones at 120, 90, 60, and 30 days before your renewal date.

Questions

Is Lumen Harbor a real insurance company?

No. It is a fictional company created for a portfolio project. It is not licensed anywhere, has no customers, and sells no insurance.

Can I buy a policy or get a quote?

No. The agent can only give you an illustrative score and plan inside the chat.

Is the score accurate?

No. The weights, rubrics, and recommendations are simplified demo content. Do not use them for real decisions about insurance, risk, or finances.

What should I tell the agent?

General answers only, such as an industry and a size range. Do not share company names, personal details, policy numbers, or anything confidential.

How was this built?

The site is plain HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript. The agent was built with Microsoft Copilot Studio and added to the page as an embedded chat.

Lumen Harbor Insurance is not a real company.

This website is a portfolio demonstration. It is not an insurance company, agency, or broker, and it is not licensed in any state or country. Nothing on this site or from the chat agent is insurance, legal, financial, or risk advice. Any resemblance to a real company is a coincidence.
